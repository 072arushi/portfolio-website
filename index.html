 Research

Published papers

Seven peer-reviewed papers with IEEE, Springer, and Wiley — spanning sentiment analysis, NLP, and deep-learning approaches to lung-cancer diagnosis.

Wiley · Expert Systems, 2024 · LLMs / NLP review

Prospect of large language models and natural language processing for lung cancer diagnosis: A systematic review

A systematic review of how large language models and NLP can support lung-cancer diagnosis — across medical imaging and the interpretation of complex clinical data.
Wiley ↗
IEEE CONECCT, 2024 · Vision transformers

Transforming Lung Cancer Diagnosis with Twin Vision Transformers

A Twin Vision Transformer (TwinViT) that classifies non-small-cell lung-cancer subtypes from CT scans using parallel processing, reaching 94.3% accuracy.
IEEE Xplore ↗
IEEE I2CT, 2024 · Deep-learning ensembles

Enhanced Efficiency in Lung Cancer Classification via Deep Learning Ensembles

VGG16 feature extraction paired with an SVM classifier on the Lung-1 dataset, separating adenocarcinoma from small-cell carcinoma at 98% accuracy.
IEEE Xplore ↗
IEEE SPIN, 2023 · Medical imaging

Pulmonary Nodules Binary Classification using CNN and LSTM

A CNN model on LIDC-IDRI CT scans for binary lung-nodule classification, achieving 83.86% accuracy.
IEEE Xplore ↗
IEEE AIST, 2022 · Deep learning

Lung Cancer Classification using Long Short Term Memory

An LSTM model (PyTorch) classifying lung nodules as benign or malignant from LIDC-IDRI and LUNA-16 CT scans, reaching 86.98% accuracy.
IEEE Xplore ↗
IEEE CICT, 2022 · Medical imaging

Fine-Tuned Framework With Transfer Learning for Pulmonary Nodule Classification

A CNN pulmonary-nodule classifier using VGG16 and ResNet50 transfer learning on LIDC-IDRI CT scans, with fine-tuning to lift accuracy to 84.16%.
IEEE Xplore ↗
Springer · ICAIST, 2021 · NLP / Sentiment analysis

Analytical Approach for Sentiment Analysis of Movie Reviews using CNN and LSTM

A study of CNN and LSTM approaches to sentiment classification of movie reviews — comparing methods, datasets, results, and limitations in NLP opinion mining.
